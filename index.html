About the workshop

The 19th Miniherpesvirus Workshop is a community-organized scientific meeting dedicated to herpesvirology, with a particular focus on early-career researchers.

Abstract submission

Abstract submission will be handled through a Google Form. Abstracts will be reviewed by the organizing committee for inclusion as talks or posters.

  • Presentation formats: talk, poster, or flash talk
  • Suggested abstract length: up to 300 words
  • Figures, tables, and references: not required

Privacy

Data submitted through the abstract submission and registration forms will be used only for organizing the Miniherpesvirus Workshop, communicating with participants, reviewing abstracts, and preparing the workshop programme. Data will be accessible only to the organizing committee and will not be shared publicly without consent.
